90-Day Plan: Driving a Unified Platform Experience

Joel Stocker

Principal Product Manager

Mission & Overview

This section provides a high-level overview of the strategic mission to create a truly unified "ControlUp ONE" experience. The goal is to build a cohesive and intuitive user journey across all products. The timeline below illustrates the shift in focus from learning and assessment towards execution and impact over this period.

Days 1-30 Learn & Assess
Days 31-60 Plan & Initiate
Days 61-90 Execute & Measure

Mission Statement

To transform our platform into a truly unified "ControlUp ONE" experience. My mission is to create a cohesive and intuitive user journey across all products and modules, ensuring that familiarity with one part of our platform empowers users to master any other, now and in the future.

Guiding Principles

Customer-Obsessed

Ground every decision in qualitative and quantitative user data.

Data-Informed

Use analytics, primarily FullStory, to identify friction, validate hypotheses, and measure impact.

Collaborate to Win

Build strong bridges with PM, R&D, UX, and GTM teams.

Execute with Impact

Focus on iterative progress and measurable wins that reduce churn.

The First 30 Days: Learn, Assess & Align

Theme: Deep Immersion & Foundational Analysis

The initial 30 days are dedicated to deep immersion and establishing the cross-functional relationships necessary to drive a unified platform strategy. This includes transitioning from my prior role, conducting a hands-on product assessment, and initiating key collaborations.

Key Actions

  • Transition & Handover

    Ensure a smooth handover of current projects and responsibilities to my former team, guaranteeing continuity.

  • Product & Data Deep Dive

    Perform a fresh, hands-on onboarding as a new user and document all findings. Analyze FullStory data for user drop-off points. Assess Pendo for in-app guidance capabilities.

  • Stakeholder Alignment & Insight Gathering

    Meet with PMs, R&D, and UX to align on the unified vision. Review existing use case documentation. Begin initial research on onboarding tools (Pendo, Userpilot, Appcues).

  • Cross-Functional Collaboration

    Partner with Shirly to understand the enhancement request project and explore opportunities for contextual, in-product feedback mechanisms.

Desired Outcomes

  • A comprehensive understanding of the current onboarding state is achieved, with key findings socialized.
  • Strong working relationships with key partners in R&D, PM, UX, and GTM are established.
  • A clear picture of high-value use cases and their current in-product implementation is formed.

The Next 30 Days (31-60): Plan, Collaborate & Initiate

Theme: Formulating the Strategy & Starting Execution

This phase shifts from learning to action. The core objective is to translate insights into a prioritized roadmap that differentiates between user segments, formalizes tooling decisions, and begins to deliver a more cohesive experience.

Key Actions

  • Strategic Planning & Roadmap

    Develop and present a prioritized roadmap for the "first mile" of the user experience. Define distinct onboarding strategies for Trial vs. Paid Customers. Lead a formal evaluation of onboarding tools (Pendo, Userpilot, Appcues) vs. building in-house.

  • Cross-Functional Collaboration

    Host workshops with PM/UX peers to align on the unified vision. Collaborate with R&D to scope initiatives. Align with Marketing on email nurture campaigns for a seamless journey.

  • Initiate First Projects

    Launch a "quick win" identified in the first 30 days to build momentum. Kick off development for the highest-priority item on the unified experience roadmap.

Desired Outcomes

  • A clear, prioritized roadmap focused on a unified onboarding experience is socialized and has stakeholder buy-in.
  • Initial development on the first major improvement has commenced.
  • A final decision on the "buy vs. build" strategy for onboarding tooling is made.

The Final 30 Days (61-90): Execute, Measure & Optimize

Theme: Delivering Value & Demonstrating Impact

The final phase is focused on execution and impact. The core objective is to ship meaningful improvements, rigorously measure their effect on user behavior, and establish a repeatable framework for continuous optimization of the unified platform experience.

Key Actions

  • Ship & Deliver

    Launch the MVP of the in-product Use Case Library. Deploy the first major unified onboarding flow improvement. Deliver "out-of-the-box" preconfigured content to combat the "blank canvas" problem.

  • Measure & Analyze

    Track the impact of launched improvements via FullStory (adoption, completion rates, etc.). Establish a tight feedback loop with UX, CS, and Support to review user behavior and qualitative feedback.

  • Set the Foundation for the Future

    Present 90-day outcomes and the next quarter's roadmap. Establish a recurring "Product Adoption Review" meeting. Begin implementation or development of the chosen onboarding/feedback platform.

Desired Outcomes

  • The first tangible improvements to the unified user experience are shipped and positively impacting key adoption indicators.
  • A clear, data-informed recommendation on the future of our in-app guidance and feedback platforms is delivered and actioned.
  • A repeatable process for collaborating with cross-functional teams on adoption-focused projects is in place.
